Niels Bohr Institute · University of Copenhagen

I study black holes through the gravitational waves they give off.

Postdoctoral researcher in the Strong Gravity group at the Niels Bohr Institute, supervised by Vitor Cardoso.

When two black holes collide, the collision sends ripples through space called gravitational waves. I study the part of the signal produced just after merger to test whether the black hole left behind behaves the way general relativity predicts.
