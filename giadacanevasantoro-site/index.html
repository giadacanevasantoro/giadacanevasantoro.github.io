Center of Gravity · Niels Bohr Institute

I work out whether the black hole general relativity predicts is the one gravitational-wave detectors actually see.

Postdoctoral researcher in the Strong Gravity group at the Center of Gravity (CoG), Niels Bohr Institute, University of Copenhagen, supervised by Vitor Cardoso. Junior Associate of the Simons Collaboration on Black Holes and Strong Gravity.

My work sits in gravitational-wave tests of general relativity, mostly through the post-merger and ringdown: black hole spectroscopy, quasinormal modes, tidal Love numbers, and what astrophysical environments do to all of the above. I lead development on pyRing, the ringdown Bayesian-inference pipeline used by the LIGO–Virgo–KAGRA Collaboration for O4. I think of myself as a data analyst before a theorist.
